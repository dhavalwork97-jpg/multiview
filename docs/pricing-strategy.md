# FGC Stream pricing strategy

FGC Stream is priced as tournament operations + broadcast infrastructure, not as a generic streaming tool.

## Recommended launch plans

| Plan | Price | Best for | Core limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trial | Free for 14 days | Evaluating FGC Stream | 2 stations |
| Organizer | ₹1,499/month | Regular local organizers | 5 stations |
| Pro | ₹3,999/month | Clubs, venues, recurring events | 12 stations |
| Event | ₹7,500/event | One-off tournaments | 16 stations |
| Enterprise | Custom | Leagues, venues, production companies | Custom |

### Trial

The trial should expose the real product rather than a crippled demo: tournament setup, participants, bracket and schedule, station assignment, Control Room, broadcast console, YouTube connection, saved RTMP destinations, and Multiview.

### Organizer — ₹1,499/month

The default self-serve plan for recurring organizers. Includes up to 5 stations, unlimited tournaments, tournament participants/teams, scheduling, station assignment and health, Control Room/rundown, YouTube Live connection, saved RTMP destinations, broadcast/HUD workflow, Multiview, public tournament pages, and basic branding.

### Pro — ₹3,999/month

For larger recurring operations. Includes up to 12 stations, everything in Organizer, multi-operator workflows, richer broadcast controls, sponsor/brand presentation, reusable event configuration, and priority support. Do not advertise analytics or integrations that are not actually production-ready.

### Event — ₹7,500/event

For organizers who run occasional events and do not want a subscription. Includes up to 16 stations, full tournament operations, broadcast setup, YouTube/RTMP destination setup, Control Room, Multiview, branding, and event-day support. Larger events can be quoted separately.

### Enterprise

Custom pricing for leagues, venues, production companies, and multi-event operators. Scope can include higher station counts, multiple organizations, dedicated support, custom integrations, and commercial terms.

## Pricing principles

- Do not charge separately for YouTube connection or saved RTMP destinations; they are core broadcast workflow features.
- Do not price by viewer count; FGC Stream operates the event rather than being the video CDN.
- Do not promise unlimited stations, analytics, VOD tooling, or provider integrations unless the corresponding production feature is real.
- Make Event the strongest option for customers who run tournaments occasionally; make Organizer the obvious default for recurring local organizers.
- Keep customer provider credentials out of pricing/configuration copy. Organizers should only see Connect YouTube or Add RTMP.
